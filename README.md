### CHEM-000244: Selecting the physical branch of self-consistent Green’s functions
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Establish criteria selecting the physical equilibrium solution among self-consistent GF2 or GW branches, while distinguishing genuine metastability from diagrammatic-truncation artifacts.
- **Status:** Multiple solutions are documented. Pokhilko and Zgid’s 2025 homotopy method tracks Dyson-equation branches, including NdNiO₂ examples, without resolving universal physical branch selection.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Prevents a converged numerical solution from receiving an unjustified thermodynamic interpretation.
- **Key works:** P. Pokhilko and D. Zgid (2025), *Homotopy continuation method for solving Dyson equation fully self-consistently: Theory and application to NdNiO2*, [DOI:10.1063/5.0288359](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0288359).
- **Related:** Nonlinear analysis: bifurcation and continuation; condensed-matter physics: metastable phases.
